Walking with Kṛṣṇa in the hall at Indraprastha, Arjuna admits he has forgotten what he was taught on the battlefield. Kṛṣṇa says he cannot say it the same way again, but teaches it once more through old stories of a brahmin and his wife, a teacher and a pupil.
