Leaving Hastināpura, Kṛṣṇa takes Karṇa into his chariot and tells him the secret: he is Kuntī’s eldest son, and so the eldest Pāṇḍava. If he comes over, he will be king, and Draupadī his wife. Karṇa already knows. He will not abandon the charioteer who raised him or the friend who honoured him, and he asks Kṛṣṇa to keep the secret until after the war.
