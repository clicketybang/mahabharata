Day 13. Sixteen-year-old Abhimanyu breaks into the spiral formation he knows how to enter but not how to leave. The breach is sealed behind him. Surrounded by Droṇa, Karṇa and others at once, his bow cut from behind, he fights on with a chariot wheel until he falls.
