Vyāsa gathers the living on the bank of the Gaṅgā, and at nightfall calls up the dead of the war from the water: sons, fathers, brothers, Karṇa among them, all without anger. For one night they are together. At dawn the dead go back into the river, and the widows who wish it follow them.
