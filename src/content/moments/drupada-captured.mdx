As his teacher’s fee, Droṇa asks his pupils to bring him Drupada. Arjuna and Bhīma defeat the Pāñcāla army and drag the king before him. Droṇa returns half the kingdom and keeps half: now they are equals, and can be friends. Drupada goes home and begins a sacrifice for a son who will kill Droṇa.
