Kings come from every quarter to Yudhiṣṭhira’s sacrifice. On Bhīṣma’s advice, the first honour goes to Kṛṣṇa. Śiśupāla of Cedi rises in rage and insults Kṛṣṇa over and over. Kṛṣṇa has promised to forgive him a hundred offences. When the hundred are passed, his discus takes Śiśupāla’s head.
