All the next day Arjuna fights his way toward Jayadratha through six great warriors and a whole army. The sun is sinking and he is still far off. Then darkness falls before its time, and the Kauravas cheer: the oath is lost. Jayadratha shows himself. Kṛṣṇa says, now, and the sun is still in the sky. Arjuna’s arrow carries Jayadratha’s head away.
