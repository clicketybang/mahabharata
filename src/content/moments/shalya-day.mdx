On the eighteenth day Śalya leads what is left of the Kaurava army. Yudhiṣṭhira, the gentlest of the brothers, rides out against him and kills him with a spear. Sahadeva hunts down Śakuni, who began it all with the dice, and kills him. By evening the Kaurava army is gone.
