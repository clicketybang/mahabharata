Śantanu falls in love with Satyavatī, the ferry-girl of the Yamunā, but her father will give her only if her son will be king. Devavrata gives up the throne, and then, so that no son of his can ever claim it, swears never to marry. The gods call him Bhīṣma, ‘the terrible’, and his father grants him the gift of choosing the hour of his death.
