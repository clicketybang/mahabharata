The fighting goes on into the night, and in the dark Bhīma’s son Ghaṭotkaca is almost unstoppable: the Kaurava army breaks before him. Duryodhana begs Karṇa to use the spear Indra gave him, the one he was keeping for Arjuna. Karṇa throws it, and Ghaṭotkaca falls, crushing a whole division as he dies. In the Pāṇḍava camp, Kṛṣṇa is glad: Arjuna is safe now.
