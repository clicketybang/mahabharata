Young Karṇa, passing himself off as a brahmin, studies with Paraśurāma on Mount Mahendra. While the sage sleeps on his lap, an insect bores into Karṇa’s thigh; he bears the pain without moving so as not to wake him. Paraśurāma wakes in blood and knows no brahmin could endure that — he curses Karṇa to forget his greatest weapon when he needs it most. Nearby, Karṇa’s stray arrow kills a brahmin’s cow; the brahmin curses that the earth will swallow his chariot wheel in his decisive battle.
