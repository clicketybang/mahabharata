Frightened by the omens, Dhṛtarāṣṭra offers Draupadī boons. She asks only that Yudhiṣṭhira be free, then that his brothers be freed with their weapons — and refuses a third boon, saying more would be greed. The Pāṇḍavas leave with everything restored.
