The Sun warns Karṇa in a dream: Indra will come disguised as a brahmin to beg his armour and earrings, and without them he can be killed. Karṇa will not refuse anyone who asks at his morning worship. Indra comes, and Karṇa cuts the armour and earrings from his own body and gives them. Shamed, Indra gives him a spear that will kill one enemy, once.
