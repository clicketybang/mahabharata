The house at Vāraṇāvata is built of lac, hemp and resin, made to burn. Vidura warns Yudhiṣṭhira in a riddle only he will understand, and sends a miner who digs a tunnel. On the night the house is set alight, the Pāṇḍavas and Kuntī escape underground. A forest woman and her five sons, asleep inside, are burned, and Hastināpura believes the Pāṇḍavas dead.
