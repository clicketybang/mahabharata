Bhīṣma arranges the marriage of the blind prince Dhṛtarāṣṭra to Gāndhārī, princess of Gāndhāra. When she learns her husband cannot see, she takes a cloth, folds it many times, and binds her own eyes. She will wear it for the rest of her life. Her brother Śakuni brings her to Hastināpura.
