Hoping to draw the Pāṇḍavas out, the Kurus raid Virāṭa’s cattle. Young prince Uttara boasts that he will beat them, takes Bṛhannaḍā as his charioteer, and panics at the sight of the army. Arjuna swaps places with him, takes his own bow from the śamī tree, and alone defeats Bhīṣma, Droṇa, Karṇa and Duryodhana. The thirteenth year, by Bhīṣma’s count, is already over.
