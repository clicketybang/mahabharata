Before the sacrifice, one king must fall: Jarāsandha of Magadha, who holds many kings captive. Kṛṣṇa, Bhīma and Arjuna enter his city disguised as brahmins and challenge him to single combat. Bhīma and Jarāsandha wrestle for thirteen days. Born in two halves joined together, Jarāsandha dies only when Bhīma tears him apart along the seam.
