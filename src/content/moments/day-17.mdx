<Beat from={0} to={0.13} title="The Seventeenth Day" dev="सप्तदशं दिनम्" cast={["arjuna", "krishna", "karna"]}>

The sun climbs over Kurukṣetra. Arjuna, his white horses driven by Kṛṣṇa, faces Karṇa, newly commander of the Kauravas, driven by King Śalya.

</Beat>

<Beat from={0.13} to={0.3} title="Gāṇḍīva and Vijaya" dev="धनुर्युद्धम्" cast={["arjuna", "karna", "krishna"]}>

Arrows darken the air. Śalya, forced to drive for Karṇa, taunts him at every turn. Karṇa looses a serpent-arrow at Arjuna’s throat; Kṛṣṇa presses the chariot into the earth, and it strikes only Arjuna’s crown.

</Beat>

<Beat from={0.3} to={0.46} title="The Whole Field" dev="कुरुक्षेत्रम्" cast={["yudhishthira", "arjuna", "bhima"]}>

Pull back, and the duel is one knot in a vast cloth. Yudhiṣṭhira, wounded by Karṇa this morning, lies in his tent — where <Jump to="tent-quarrel">he and Arjuna quarrelled bitterly</Jump> only hours ago. And at the field’s western edge, Bhīma has found the man he has hunted for thirteen years.

</Beat>

<Beat from={0.46} to={0.62} title="Bhīma and Duḥśāsana" dev="भीमदुःशासनयुद्धम्" cast={["bhima", "dushasana"]}>

Duḥśāsana faces the son of the wind. Bhīma remembers <Jump to="dice-hall" anchor="vow">the vow he swore in the dice hall</Jump>. His mace falls once, and Duḥśāsana goes down.

</Beat>

<Beat from={0.62} to={0.74} title="The Vow Kept" dev="प्रतिज्ञापूर्तिः" warn="Content: graphic violence" cast={["bhima", "dushasana"]}>

Bhīma kneels on his enemy’s chest, tears it open and drinks, roaring that it tastes sweeter than milk or honey. The Kaurava ranks scatter in horror.

</Beat>

<Beat from={0.74} to={0.88} title="The Wheel" dev="रथचक्रम्" cast={["karna", "krishna", "arjuna"]}>

Karṇa’s wheel sinks into the earth — he remembers <Jump to="karna-curse">the brahmin who cursed him</Jump> long ago. He calls on Arjuna to honour the rules of war. Kṛṣṇa answers: where was dharma when Draupadī stood in the hall? When a boy was surrounded <Jump to="abhimanyu">in the Cakravyūha</Jump>?

</Beat>

<Beat from={0.88} to={1.01} title="The Añjalika" dev="अञ्जलिकः" cast={["arjuna", "karna"]}>

Arjuna draws an arrow that shines like the sun Karṇa was born from. Here the cloth pauses. Follow a soul, or go to a place — the story moves only forward.

</Beat>
