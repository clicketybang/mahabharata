Eleven armies march for Duryodhana and seven for the Pāṇḍavas, and they camp facing each other at Kurukṣetra. Bhīṣma accepts command of the Kauravas, saying he will kill ten thousand a day but never a Pāṇḍava. Karṇa refuses to fight while Bhīṣma leads. Dhṛṣṭadyumna takes command of the Pāṇḍava army.
