The queen’s brother Kīcaka, commander of the army, wants the maid Draupadī, and when she refuses he kicks her in front of the court. Yudhiṣṭhira, in disguise, can only tell her to be patient. At night she goes to the cook. Bhīma waits in the dark dance-hall in her place, and when Kīcaka comes, kills him with his bare hands.
