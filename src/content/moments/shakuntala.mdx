Hunting in the forest, King Duṣyanta comes to Kaṇva’s hermitage and marries Śakuntalā there by mutual consent. Years later she brings their son to court, and the king pretends not to know her until a voice from the sky confirms her words. The boy, Bharata, gives the land and the line their name.
