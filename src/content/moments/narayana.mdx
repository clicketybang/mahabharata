Mad with grief and fury, Aśvatthāmā looses the Nārāyaṇa weapon on the Pāṇḍava army: a rain of fire that grows stronger against anyone who resists. Kṛṣṇa calls on every warrior to drop his weapons and step down from his chariot. Bhīma refuses and is nearly burned alive until Kṛṣṇa and Arjuna drag him down. The weapon passes over the unresisting army.
