King Śantanu marries a woman who makes one condition: he must never question what she does. She drowns seven newborn sons in the Gaṅgā. When he stops her from drowning the eighth, she reveals she is the river goddess, and leaves. The boy, Devavrata, is returned to his father grown and taught.
