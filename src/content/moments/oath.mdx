Arjuna comes back at dusk to silence, and learns how his son died: trapped inside the formation, and Jayadratha holding the gap shut. In grief he swears that if Jayadratha is alive at sunset tomorrow, he will walk into the fire himself. The Kaurava camp hears the oath and shouts with joy; Jayadratha wants to flee, and is persuaded to stay, guarded by the whole army.
