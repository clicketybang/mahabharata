With both armies ready, Yudhiṣṭhira takes off his armour, lays down his weapons and walks across the field toward the enemy. His brothers follow, alarmed. He touches the feet of Bhīṣma, Droṇa, Kṛpa and Śalya in turn and asks their blessing to fight them. Each gives it, and each tells him how he can be beaten.
