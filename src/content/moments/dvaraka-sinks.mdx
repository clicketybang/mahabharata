Arjuna comes to Dvārakā to take its women and children to safety. As they leave, the sea rises over the empty city. On the road, cowherd robbers attack the caravan, and Arjuna finds he can hardly string Gāṇḍīva; the divine weapons no longer come when he calls. Vyāsa tells him their time has passed.
