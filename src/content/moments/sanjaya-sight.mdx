Vyāsa visits his blind son and offers him sight to watch the war. Dhṛtarāṣṭra refuses: he does not want to see his family die, only to hear of it. So Vyāsa gives the sight to Sañjaya instead. From here on, Sañjaya tells the war to the king, day by day, as he sees it.
