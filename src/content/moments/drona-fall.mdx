Droṇa kills Drupada and Virāṭa on the fifteenth day, and no one can stand before him. Kṛṣṇa says the only way is to make him put down his weapons. Bhīma kills an elephant named Aśvatthāmā and shouts that Aśvatthāmā is dead. Droṇa will believe only Yudhiṣṭhira, who has never lied. Yudhiṣṭhira says it, adding ‘the elephant’ under his breath. Droṇa lays down his arms, and Dhṛṣṭadyumna cuts off his head.
