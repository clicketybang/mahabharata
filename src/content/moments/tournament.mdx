The princes show the city what Droṇa has taught them, and Arjuna outshines them all. Then a stranger in armour and earrings walks in and matches every feat. Kṛpa asks his lineage; Karṇa, a charioteer’s son, has no answer. Duryodhana makes him king of Aṅga on the spot, and wins a friend for life. In the stands, Kuntī recognises her son, and faints.
