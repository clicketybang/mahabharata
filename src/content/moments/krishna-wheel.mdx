Bhīṣma cuts down the Pāṇḍava army day after day, and Arjuna, still unwilling, fights him gently. Kṛṣṇa, who swore not to take up arms, leaps from the chariot and runs at Bhīṣma with a weapon in his hand. Bhīṣma lowers his bow and welcomes death at his hands. Arjuna catches Kṛṣṇa, and promises to fight in earnest.
