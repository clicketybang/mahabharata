Arjuna comes to see his wounded brother. Yudhiṣṭhira, learning Karṇa still lives, tells him in shame to give his Gāṇḍīva to someone worthier. Arjuna had vowed to kill anyone who said that, and draws his sword. Kṛṣṇa steps between them: truth must serve life, not destroy it. Arjuna rides out to finish the day.
