In Mathurā, the tyrant Kaṃsa has imprisoned his sister Devakī and her husband Vasudeva, and kills each of their children at birth. When the eighth, Kṛṣṇa, is born, the guards sleep and the doors open. Vasudeva carries the baby across the flooded Yamunā to the cowherds of Gokula, where he grows up.
