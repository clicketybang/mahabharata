Yudhiṣṭhira is named heir, and the people praise him in the streets. Duryodhana goes to his father: if the kingdom passes to Pāṇḍu’s line, his sons will be servants forever. The king, torn, agrees to send the Pāṇḍavas away to a festival at Vāraṇāvata, where Duryodhana has had a house built for them.
