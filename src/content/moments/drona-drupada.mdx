As boys, Droṇa and Drupada studied together, and Drupada promised to share his kingdom one day. Now a poor brahmin with a hungry son, Droṇa comes to King Drupada’s court and greets him as a friend. Drupada laughs: a king has no friends among beggars. Droṇa leaves without a word, and goes to Hastināpura.
