Bhīṣma asks for a pillow, and Arjuna gives him three arrows. He asks for water, and Arjuna shoots into the earth, and a spring rises to his lips. That night, when the others have gone, Karṇa comes at last. Bhīṣma tells him he has always known whose son he is, and begs him to make peace. Karṇa asks only for leave to fight.
