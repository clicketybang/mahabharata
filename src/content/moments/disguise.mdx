For the thirteenth year they must not be found. They hide their weapons in a śamī tree near a cremation ground and enter King Virāṭa’s service: Yudhiṣṭhira as a dice-playing courtier, Bhīma as a cook, Arjuna as Bṛhannaḍā, a eunuch dancing-teacher, Nakula as a horse-keeper, Sahadeva as a cowherd, and Draupadī as the queen’s maid.
