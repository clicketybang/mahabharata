Staying on in Indraprastha, Duryodhana walks through Maya’s hall. He lifts his clothes to cross a floor he takes for water, then walks straight into a pool he takes for floor. Bhīma, Arjuna and the twins laugh at him, and so do the servants. He goes home sick with envy.
