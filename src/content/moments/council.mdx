After the wedding, the allies sit in council. Kṛṣṇa asks only for what is just: the Pāṇḍavas’ half of the kingdom. Balarāma blames Yudhiṣṭhira for gambling it away and urges a gentle embassy. Drupada says Duryodhana will never yield and they must prepare for war. They do both: an envoy goes to Hastināpura, and messengers go out to every king.
