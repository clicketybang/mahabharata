Out hunting, King Pāṇḍu shoots a deer in the act of mating. It is a sage who had taken a deer’s form, and as he dies he curses Pāṇḍu: if he ever lies with a woman, he will die. Pāṇḍu gives up the kingdom and goes into the mountains with his two wives, Kuntī and Mādrī.
