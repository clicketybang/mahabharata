Dhṛtarāṣṭra sends Sañjaya to Upaplavya with a message: war is evil, and a good man would rather beg than fight his kin. He offers peace but no land. Yudhiṣṭhira answers that he would settle for five villages. Kṛṣṇa answers that a warrior who lets injustice stand is not keeping dharma, he is abandoning it.
