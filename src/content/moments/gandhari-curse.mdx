The women come out to Kurukṣetra to find their dead. Through her blindfold, Gāndhārī sees the whole field: her hundred sons, her daughter’s husband, the widows. She turns to Kṛṣṇa: he could have stopped this and did not. She curses him: in thirty-six years his own clan will destroy itself, and he will die alone. Kṛṣṇa answers that he knew this already.
