Arjuna goes alone to the Himālaya to win weapons from the gods. As he does penance on Indrakīla, a boar charges him, and he and a wild hunter shoot it at the same moment. They quarrel over the kill and fight; Arjuna’s arrows vanish into the hunter, and in the end he is beaten. The hunter is Śiva. Pleased, he gives Arjuna the Pāśupata weapon.
