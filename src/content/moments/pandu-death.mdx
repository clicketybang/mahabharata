One spring day Pāṇḍu sees Mādrī alone in the forest, forgets the curse, and takes her in his arms. He dies at once. Mādrī follows him onto the funeral fire, leaving her twins to Kuntī, who brings all five boys down from the mountains to Hastināpura.
