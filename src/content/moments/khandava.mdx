The fire god Agni, sick from too much ghee, asks Arjuna and Kṛṣṇa to let him eat the Khāṇḍava forest. With a new bow, Gāṇḍīva, and a discus, they guard its edges for days while everything inside burns, and turn back even Indra’s rain. Of the creatures who try to flee, only a few are spared, among them the asura architect Maya.
