Droṇa refuses to teach Ekalavya, a Niṣāda prince. The boy makes a clay image of Droṇa in the forest and teaches himself before it, until he shoots better than Arjuna. Droṇa, who has promised Arjuna that no one will surpass him, asks Ekalavya for his teacher’s fee: his right thumb. Ekalavya cuts it off and gives it.
