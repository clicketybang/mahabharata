The Pāṇḍavas crown Parīkṣit and walk north into the mountains with Draupadī, and a dog follows them. One by one they fall: Draupadī first, then Sahadeva, Nakula, Arjuna and Bhīma, and each time Yudhiṣṭhira says why and does not look back. Indra arrives in his chariot to take him to heaven, but not the dog. Yudhiṣṭhira will not abandon a creature who trusts him. The dog becomes Dharma, his father.
