Fetching a flower for Draupadī on Mount Gandhamādana, Bhīma finds an old monkey lying across the path and tells him to move. Too weak, says the monkey; just lift my tail aside. Bhīma cannot move it at all. The monkey is Hanumān, his elder brother by the Wind, who shows him the size he took to leap to Laṅkā, and blesses him.
