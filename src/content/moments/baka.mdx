Hidden as brahmins in the village of Ekacakrā, the family hears their host weeping: it is his house’s turn to send a cartload of food and a person to the demon Baka. Kuntī offers one of her own sons instead. Yudhiṣṭhira is appalled; Kuntī answers that she knows Bhīma’s strength. Bhīma eats the food himself and kills the demon.
