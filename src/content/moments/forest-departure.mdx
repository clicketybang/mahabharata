After fifteen years in Yudhiṣṭhira’s court, Dhṛtarāṣṭra asks leave to end his days in the forest, and Gāndhārī goes with him. Kuntī goes too. Her sons beg her to stay and enjoy the kingdom she pushed them to win; she says she pushed them to their duty, not to her comfort, and walks out of the city behind the blind king and queen.
