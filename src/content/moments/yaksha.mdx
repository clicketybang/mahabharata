Thirsty after a chase, the brothers go one by one to a forest lake. A crane warns each to answer its questions before drinking; each drinks anyway and falls dead. Yudhiṣṭhira answers: what is quicker than the wind? the mind. What is the greatest wonder? that every day people die, and the living think they never will. Offered one brother back, he chooses Nakula, so that Mādrī too has a living son. The crane is Dharma, his father, and all four rise.
