On the third day after the raid, the five walk into Virāṭa’s hall in royal clothes and sit on the thrones. The king is amazed, then delighted, and offers his daughter Uttarā to Arjuna. Arjuna, who taught her dance as a father would, asks her hand for his son instead. Kṛṣṇa arrives from Dvārakā with Abhimanyu, and the wedding brings the allies together.
