To thank Arjuna for his life, Maya builds the Pāṇḍavas an assembly hall unlike any on earth, with pools that look like floors and floors that look like pools. It takes fourteen months. When it is done, the sage Nārada visits and tells Yudhiṣṭhira that his father in heaven wants him to perform the royal sacrifice.
