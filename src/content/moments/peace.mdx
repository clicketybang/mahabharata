Kṛṣṇa goes himself to Hastināpura as the Pāṇḍavas’ envoy. Before the whole assembly he asks for their half of the kingdom, then for five villages. Bhīṣma, Droṇa, Vidura and even Gāndhārī beg Duryodhana to accept. He refuses to give up as much land as a needle’s point would cover, and plots to seize the envoy. Kṛṣṇa shows the hall a vision of his cosmic form, and walks out free.
