In the Pāṇḍava camp, Arjuna turns on his brother: their teacher trusted him, and he killed him with a lie; the shame will last as long as the world. Bhīma says Droṇa was a brahmin who had abandoned his duty and was killing thousands. Dhṛṣṭadyumna says he only did what he was born to do. The brothers part without agreeing, and the war goes on.
