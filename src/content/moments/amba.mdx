To find wives for his young half-brother, Bhīṣma carries off the three princesses of Kāśī from their own svayaṃvara. The eldest, Ambā, says her heart is already given to King Śālva; she is sent to him, and he will not take her back. Rejected by everyone, she turns her whole life toward one end: Bhīṣma’s death.
