In the forest, Bhīma keeps watch while the others sleep. The rākṣasī Hiḍimbā, sent by her brother to catch them, falls in love with Bhīma instead. Bhīma kills the brother. With Kuntī’s blessing, Hiḍimbā and Bhīma live together for a time, and their son Ghaṭotkaca is born, grows up in a day, and promises to come whenever his father calls.
