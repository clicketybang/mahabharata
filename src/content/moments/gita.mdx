Arjuna asks Kṛṣṇa to drive between the armies, and sees grandfathers, teachers, cousins and friends. His bow slips from his hand: he will not kill them for a kingdom. Kṛṣṇa answers him at length, about duty, the self that does not die, and action done without craving for its fruits. At the end, Arjuna’s doubt is gone, and he picks up his bow.
