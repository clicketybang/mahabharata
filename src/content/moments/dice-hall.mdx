<Beat alt="A long assembly hall of ochre pillars and garlanded arches. The blind king sits on a red throne at the centre; elders sit to the left; the Pāṇḍava brothers stand and sit before a dice board." from={0} to={0.14} title="The Hall" dev="सभा" cast={["yudhishthira", "duryodhana", "bhima", "arjuna"]}>

Hastināpura’s assembly is full: the blind king on his throne, Bhīṣma and Droṇa among the elders, the Kaurava princes, the five Pāṇḍavas. Yudhiṣṭhira has been invited to play. A king, he believes, may not refuse a challenge.

</Beat>

<Beat alt="Close on the dice board: Yudhiṣṭhira and Śakuni sit facing each other as two white dice tumble through the air between them." from={0.14} to={0.34} title="The Throws" dev="अक्षाः" cast={["yudhishthira", "duryodhana"]}>

Śakuni throws for Duryodhana. Yudhiṣṭhira stakes pearls, gold, chariots, his army, his kingdom — and loses each. Then his brothers, one by one. Then himself. Then, at Śakuni’s prompting, Draupadī.

</Beat>

<Beat alt="At the right of the hall, through a dark doorway, Duḥśāsana drags Draupadī in by her loose hair; her hands are raised." from={0.34} to={0.5} title="The Summons" dev="आह्वानम्" warn="Content: assault" cast={["draupadi", "dushasana", "duryodhana"]}>

A messenger is sent to fetch her. Draupadī sends back a question instead. Duḥśāsana drags her into the hall by her hair, in a single garment.

</Beat>

<Beat alt="Draupadī stands in the doorway with her hair unbound, facing the seated court." from={0.5} to={0.66} title="Draupadī’s Question" dev="द्रौपदीप्रश्नः" cast={["draupadi"]}>

She asks the elders: did Yudhiṣṭhira lose himself first? If he was already a slave, how could he stake her? It is a question of law, and the hall cannot answer it.

</Beat>

<Beat alt="The left of the hall: the elders sit silent with their white beards, Yudhiṣṭhira seated beside them, Arjuna and Bhīma standing." from={0.66} to={0.78} title="The Silence" dev="मौनम्" cast={["karna", "draupadi"]}>

Bhīṣma says dharma is subtle. Droṇa is silent. Only young Vikarṇa says she was not lawfully won. Karṇa — who, unknown to all, carries <Jump to="karna-curse">a brahmin’s curse</Jump> — mocks her as a woman with five husbands.

</Beat>

<Beat alt="Bhīma, in blue, stands with his fist raised high, glaring across the hall." from={0.78} to={1.01} title="Bhīma’s Vow" dev="भीमप्रतिज्ञा" cast={["bhima", "duryodhana", "dushasana"]}>

Bhīma rises and swears before gods and men: he will break Duryodhana’s thigh in battle, and tear open Duḥśāsana’s chest and drink his blood. The words will wait thirteen years <Jump to="day-17" at={0.5}>to be kept</Jump>.

</Beat>
