On their first night out of Hastināpura, the exiles camp beneath a great banyan on the Gaṅgā, followed by brahmins who will not leave them. Yudhiṣṭhira cannot feed them. He stands in the river and praises the Sun, who gives him a copper vessel: until Draupadī has eaten each day, it will never be empty.
