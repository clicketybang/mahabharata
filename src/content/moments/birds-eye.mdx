Droṇa sets a wooden bird in a tree and asks each pupil, bow drawn, what he sees. Yudhiṣṭhira sees the tree, the bird, his teacher and his brothers. One after another the princes give the same answer, and Droṇa sends them away. Arjuna says he sees only the bird’s head. ‘Shoot,’ says Droṇa, and the head falls.
