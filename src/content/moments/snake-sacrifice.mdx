To avenge his father, King Janamejaya holds a great sacrifice that draws every serpent in the world into the fire. Takṣaka clings to Indra’s throne, and both begin to fall toward the flames. The young brahmin Āstīka, half nāga himself, wins a boon from the king and asks for the sacrifice to stop. It stops.
