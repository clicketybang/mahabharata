Hunting, King Parīkṣit grows angry with a sage who will not answer him, and drapes a dead snake around the silent man’s neck. The sage’s son curses the king to die of a snakebite in seven days. Parīkṣit hides in a tower guarded on every side, and the serpent king Takṣaka reaches him hidden as a worm in a fruit.
