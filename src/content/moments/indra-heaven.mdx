Indra brings his son to heaven, where Arjuna spends five years learning the weapons of the gods, and music and dance from the gandharvas. The apsaras Urvaśī comes to him at night; he refuses her, because she was once the wife of his ancestor and is like a mother to him. Insulted, she curses him to live a year as a eunuch. Indra makes the curse a gift: it will hide him in the thirteenth year.
