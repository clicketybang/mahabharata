The Pāṇḍavas find the lake and shame Duryodhana out of it. Yudhiṣṭhira makes a reckless offer: fight any one of us, with any weapon, and if you win, the kingdom is yours. Duryodhana chooses Bhīma, and the mace. Balarāma arrives to watch his two pupils. Duryodhana is the better fighter, until Kṛṣṇa signals by striking his own thigh. Bhīma breaks the rule of the duel and smashes Duryodhana’s thighs, as he swore he would in the dice hall.
