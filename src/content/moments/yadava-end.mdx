Thirty-six years after the war, Gāndhārī’s curse comes due. At a festival on the shore at Prabhāsa, the Yādava chiefs drink and quarrel over the night raid. Sātyaki kills Kṛtavarman, and then every man picks up the reeds by the water, which turn into iron clubs in their hands. They kill one another, and Balarāma, sitting apart, leaves his body in meditation.
