On a pilgrimage, Arjuna comes to Dvārakā and falls in love with Kṛṣṇa’s sister Subhadrā. Kṛṣṇa advises him to carry her off, as warriors may. Balarāma is furious until Kṛṣṇa calms him, and the marriage is celebrated. Their son will be Abhimanyu.
