Arjuna and Duryodhana both come to Dvārakā to ask for Kṛṣṇa’s help, and find him asleep. Duryodhana sits by his head; Arjuna stands at his feet, and so is the first one Kṛṣṇa sees on waking. Kṛṣṇa offers a choice: his whole army, or himself, unarmed, who will not fight. Arjuna chooses Kṛṣṇa. Duryodhana leaves delighted with the army.
