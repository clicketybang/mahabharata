On the tenth day, Śikhaṇḍī rides before Arjuna, and Bhīṣma will not raise his bow against him. Arjuna’s arrows pour past Śikhaṇḍī until there is no space on Bhīṣma’s body without one. He falls, and the arrows hold him off the ground. Both armies stop. He has the gift of choosing his death, and he chooses to wait for the sun to turn north.
