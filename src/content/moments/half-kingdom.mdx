Now allied to Pāñcāla, the Pāṇḍavas cannot be ignored. Bhīṣma, Droṇa and Vidura counsel peace, and the king divides the kingdom. Duryodhana keeps Hastināpura; the Pāṇḍavas are given Khāṇḍavaprastha, a wild and empty tract by the Yamunā.
