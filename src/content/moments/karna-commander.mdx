Karṇa takes command at last. He asks for one thing: a charioteer as good as Kṛṣṇa, which means Śalya, king of Madra. Śalya is outraged to drive a charioteer’s son, and agrees only on the condition that he may say whatever he likes. Secretly he has promised Yudhiṣṭhira to dishearten Karṇa in battle.
