Kuntī finds Karṇa at his worship on the Gaṅgā and waits in his shadow until he finishes. She tells him she is his mother and asks him to join his brothers. The Sun himself speaks from the sky to agree. Karṇa answers that she abandoned him when it mattered. Still, he gives her a gift: he will not kill four of her sons. Only Arjuna. Either way, she will have five.
