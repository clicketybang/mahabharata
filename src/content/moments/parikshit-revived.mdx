Uttarā’s son, struck by Aśvatthāmā’s weapon in the womb, is born dead. The women cry out to Kṛṣṇa. He takes the child and swears by every truth he has ever spoken, and the child begins to breathe. He is named Parīkṣit, and he is the last of the Kuru line.
