While the brothers are out hunting, King Jayadratha of Sindhu finds Draupadī alone in the Kāmyaka forest and carries her off by force. The Pāṇḍavas chase his army down. For their sister Duḥśalā’s sake they spare his life, but Bhīma shaves his head in five tufts and makes him declare himself their slave. He goes to Śiva and asks for one thing: to hold back the Pāṇḍavas once.
