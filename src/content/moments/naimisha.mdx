Long after, the bard Ugraśravas comes to the sages gathered for a twelve-year sacrifice in the Naimiṣa forest. They ask him for a story. He says he heard one at Janamejaya’s sacrifice, as Vaiśampāyana told it, and begins: the whole Mahābhārata, which is where this telling, too, began.
