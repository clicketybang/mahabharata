Pāṇḍu cannot father children, so Kuntī uses her mantra again, this time for her husband: Dharma gives her Yudhiṣṭhira, the Wind gives Bhīma, Indra gives Arjuna. She shares the mantra once with Mādrī, who calls the twin Aśvins and bears twins, Nakula and Sahadeva.
