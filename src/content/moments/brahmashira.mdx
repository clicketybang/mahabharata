Draupadī, mourning her sons, will not eat until Aśvatthāmā is punished. The Pāṇḍavas track him to Vyāsa’s hermitage, where he looses the Brahmaśiras weapon. Arjuna answers with his own. The sages step between the two fires. Arjuna can call his back; Aśvatthāmā cannot, and turns it on the unborn child in Uttarā’s womb. Kṛṣṇa curses him to wander the earth alone for three thousand years, and he gives up the jewel in his forehead.
