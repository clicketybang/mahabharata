After two years with child, Gāndhārī gives birth to a hard lump of flesh. Vyāsa divides it into a hundred and one parts and has them kept in jars of ghee. The first to be born is Duryodhana: at his birth, jackals and donkeys howl. Vidura begs the king to abandon the child for the family’s sake. Dhṛtarāṣṭra cannot.
