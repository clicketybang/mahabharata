Drupada has set a test for his daughter’s hand: string a huge bow and hit a target through a turning wheel. King after king fails. Karṇa rises to try, and here tellings differ: either Draupadī refuses a charioteer’s son, or the bow beats him by a hair. Then a young brahmin strings the bow and hits the mark. In the uproar Kṛṣṇa watches him and his companions, and knows them: the Pāṇḍavas are alive.
