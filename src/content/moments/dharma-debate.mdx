In the Dvaita forest, Draupadī asks Yudhiṣṭhira how he can stay calm while his brothers live in bark clothes; if the gods allow this, she says, they are not just. Bhīma says the same with less patience: thirteen years is long enough to lose everything. Yudhiṣṭhira answers that he gave his word, and that dharma is followed for its own sake, not for its rewards.
