Kṛṣṇa brings Yudhiṣṭhira to Bhīṣma, still lying on his arrows, to learn how to rule. For days the grandsire teaches: on the duties of a king, on what to do in times of disaster, on liberation, on giving. Kṛṣṇa takes away his pain so that he can speak.
