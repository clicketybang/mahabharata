Both of Satyavatī’s royal sons die without heirs. She calls on Vyāsa, her son from before her marriage, to father children for the line. One widow shuts her eyes at the sight of the wild sage, and her son Dhṛtarāṣṭra is born blind. The other goes pale, and her son Pāṇḍu is pale. A servant woman meets him without fear, and bears Vidura.
