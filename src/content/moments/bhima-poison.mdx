At a river picnic, Duryodhana feeds Bhīma poisoned food, ties him up while he sleeps, and throws him into the Gaṅgā. Bhīma sinks to the world of the nāgas, who wake him with their bites, give him a drink of great strength, and send him home stronger than before.
