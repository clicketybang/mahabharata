Balarāma raises his plough to strike Bhīma for a blow below the navel. Kṛṣṇa holds him back: Bhīma swore that vow before them all, and Duryodhana broke worse rules first. Balarāma leaves for Dvārakā in disgust, saying Duryodhana will be remembered as the one who fought fairly.
