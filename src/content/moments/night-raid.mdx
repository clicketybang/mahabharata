Aśvatthāmā lies awake under a tree and watches an owl kill sleeping crows. That night he enters the Pāṇḍava camp while Kṛpa and Kṛtavarman guard the gate. He strangles Dhṛṣṭadyumna in his bed and kills everyone he finds: Śikhaṇḍī, Draupadī’s five sons, the sleeping army. The Pāṇḍavas, with Kṛṣṇa, are camped elsewhere that night, and survive.
