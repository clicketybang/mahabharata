Following the horse of Yudhiṣṭhira’s great sacrifice, Arjuna comes to Maṇipura, where his son Babhruvāhana is king. The boy greets him humbly, and Arjuna scorns him for not fighting. Urged on by Ulūpī, Babhruvāhana fights his father and kills him. Ulūpī then brings Arjuna back to life with a jewel of the nāgas.
