In King Kuntibhoja’s house, the girl Kuntī serves a difficult sage so well that he gives her a mantra to call any god. Curious, she tries it on the Sun, and he gives her a son born in armour and earrings. Afraid and unmarried, she sets the baby adrift on the river in a basket.
