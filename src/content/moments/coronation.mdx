Yudhiṣṭhira wants to renounce the kingdom and go to the forest; his brothers, Draupadī and the sages talk him out of it, one argument at a time. He enters Hastināpura and is crowned, and he gives Dhṛtarāṣṭra the place of honour as the head of the family.
