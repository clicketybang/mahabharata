Visiting the elders in the forest, Yudhiṣṭhira glimpses Vidura, naked and wasted by fasting, and follows him. Vidura stops against a tree and looks at him, and passes his life into Yudhiṣṭhira, eye to eye: Dharma returning to Dharma’s son.
