Sañjaya’s report will be given in the morning, and the old king cannot sleep. He calls Vidura, who talks with him through the night about what makes a person wise and what makes a king fall: anger, greed, flattery, the inability to give back what is not one’s own. Dhṛtarāṣṭra agrees with everything, and changes nothing.
