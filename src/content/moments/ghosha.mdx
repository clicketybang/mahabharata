Duryodhana rides out to Dvaitavana in splendour, to let the exiles see his wealth. A gandharva king picks a fight with him at the lake, routs his army and Karṇa, and carries Duryodhana off in chains. Yudhiṣṭhira sends Arjuna and Bhīma to free their cousin. Rescued by the men he came to mock, Duryodhana wants to starve himself to death.
