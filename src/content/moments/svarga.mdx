In heaven Yudhiṣṭhira finds Duryodhana enthroned in glory, and his own brothers nowhere. He is led to them through a dark place of suffering, and hears their voices begging him to stay. He says he would rather stay with them in hell than be in heaven without them. The darkness vanishes: it was an illusion, and a last test. He bathes in the heavenly Gaṅgā, leaves his human body, and joins them all, Karṇa among them.
